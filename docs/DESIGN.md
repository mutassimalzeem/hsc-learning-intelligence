# Product Design Principles

## Personality
Calm, focused, encouraging and academically credible. Avoid childish visual treatment, shame, public rankings and manipulative engagement mechanics.

## Student journey
- Explain the purpose and expected duration of diagnostics.
- Use clear, unambiguous questions.
- Show strengths and actionable gaps.
- Distinguish observed evidence from tentative inference.
- Explain why the next activity is relevant.
- Keep repair activities focused.
- Verify with a different context/item and explain the next step.

## Tutor journey
Prioritize batch patterns and teaching actions. Apply aggregation thresholds. Restrict individual evidence to authorized tutors. Make assignment and progress workflows clear.

## Visual direction
Use readable high-contrast neutral surfaces, restrained accent, consistent spacing and typography, visible focus, and semantic status indicators. Final brand tokens remain undecided.

## Accessibility
Keyboard support, semantic structure, screen-reader labels, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, text equivalents for diagrams, reduced motion, and responsive checks at narrow mobile, tablet and desktop widths.

## Language
Validate Bangla, English or bilingual explanations with the pilot. Preserve scientific notation and terminology consistently.
