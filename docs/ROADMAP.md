# Six-Month Roadmap — Planning Baseline

Timing is a hypothesis; adjust to founder capacity, teacher access and pilot availability.

1. **Month 1 — Discovery:** interviews, competitor/substitute research, confirm syllabus and Mechanics scope, concept map, pilot partner and consent. Gate: validated problem and feasible wedge.
2. **Month 2 — Content/diagnosis prototype:** teacher-reviewed items, prototype report/intervention, evidence semantics, student think-aloud. Gate: students and teachers understand/trust bounded output.
3. **Month 3 — Technical/student slice:** identity/consent, diagnostic session, evidence capture, gap report, tests/audit. Gate: one complete student journey.
4. **Month 4 — Repair/tutor:** intervention, independent verification, reassessment, tutor batch view and assignment, authorization tests. Gate: end-to-end student and tutor workflows.
5. **Month 5 — Pilot:** small cohort, baseline, intervention, verification, delayed reassessment, feedback and teacher adjudication. Gate: usable evidence of quality and value.
6. **Month 6 — Decide:** fix blockers, evaluate false positives/outcomes, pricing/retention, security/privacy readiness. Decide expand, narrow, pivot or stop.

Two-founder ownership is not confirmed. A tentative split is backend/diagnosis/AI/data/deployment and frontend/UX/tutor workflow/pilot operations. Confirm strengths and availability before assigning.
