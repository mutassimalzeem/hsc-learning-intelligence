# Curriculum and Content Operations

Official current HSC curriculum and qualified subject-teacher review are the source of truth. Verify board(s), syllabus version, textbook edition, paper/chapter boundaries, exam conventions and content rights before authoring.

## Concept map
Represent subject/paper, chapter/topic, concept/subconcept, learning objective, prerequisites, misconception hypotheses, distinguishing evidence, linked questions and interventions. Teacher-review prerequisite edges.

## Question item specification
Stable ID/version; curriculum reference; concept/objective tags; stem/format; correct answer and rationale; distractor rationale; diagnostic hypotheses; difficulty; author/reviewer; review status; revision history; accessibility/language notes.

## Review
Draft → subject review → ambiguity check → answer-key verification → diagnostic suitability review → pilot → revision → approved release.

A question can be suitable for practice but unsuitable for diagnosing a unique misconception. Record this explicitly.

## Initial candidate
Planning range 15–25 concepts and 50–100 reviewed Mechanics items. Validate against syllabus and teacher capacity. Version content so historical attempts remain interpretable.
