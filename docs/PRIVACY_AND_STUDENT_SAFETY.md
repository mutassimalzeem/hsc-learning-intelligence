# Privacy and Student Safety

The product may serve minors. Privacy and safeguarding are core requirements.

- Collect minimum data needed for learning; explain purpose, visibility, retention and sharing.
- Design appropriate student/guardian consent and withdrawal processes with legal review.
- Provide access, correction and deletion workflows.
- Do not sell student data or use it for unrelated advertising.
- Avoid public rankings and profiling.
- Classify account, consent, assessment, diagnostic, tutor, analytics and support data. Define purpose, access, retention and deletion for each.
- Tutors see only assigned cohorts and permitted details. Aggregate reports need thresholds to prevent re-identification.
- Review third-party and AI provider processing, retention, training-use settings, security and regional handling.
- Minimize or pseudonymize external AI context; document data flows.
- Provide a route to report harmful or inaccurate content and assign incident ownership.

Before pilot, obtain appropriate legal/privacy review for Bangladesh and any other relevant jurisdiction. This document is not legal advice.
