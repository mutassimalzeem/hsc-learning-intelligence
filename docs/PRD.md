# Product Requirements Document

**Status:** Draft; validate with users and teachers.  
**Working name:** HSC Learning Intelligence.

## Problem hypothesis
Students practise questions and receive marks but may not understand the concept or misconception behind repeated mistakes. Tutors see scores but lack scalable evidence about why a batch struggles. Validate this through interviews; do not treat it as proven market research.

## Users
- HSC Science students in Bangladesh, potentially under 18.
- Tutors/coaching-centre teachers managing one or more batches.
- Subject teachers reviewing curriculum, questions and diagnostic validity.

## Promise
Student: understand the reason behind mistakes and know what to work on next. Tutor: see what learners misunderstand, not only their scores. Never promise guaranteed exam results or certainty unsupported by evidence.

## Core loop
Diagnose → Explain → Repair → Verify → Reassess.

## MVP requirements
- Responsive web/PWA.
- Student onboarding and appropriate consent.
- Teacher-reviewed, versioned curriculum and questions.
- Short diagnostic assessment and raw evidence capture.
- Explainable concept-gap report with uncertainty.
- Targeted reviewed explanation/practice.
- Independent verification using different or varied items.
- Delayed reassessment and progress history.
- Tutor batch-level misconception patterns and remediation assignment.
- Auditability of content and diagnostic configuration.

## Out of scope
Full video library, huge question bank, general AI chatbot, live classes, social feed, leaderboards, multiple subjects/boards, native apps before web validation, high-stakes automated grading.

## Principles
A single wrong MCQ is not proof of a specific misconception. Distinguish observation from inference. Use supportive non-shaming feedback. Complement teachers and existing content providers. Minimize student data.

## Metrics
Diagnostic agreement with teacher review; false-positive rate; verification and delayed retention; assessment/intervention completion; student clarity; tutor time saved and repeat use; willingness to pay, retention and cost per active learner. Do not optimize raw question volume or time-on-app at the expense of learning.

## Open questions
Exact board/syllabus, language, initial chapter scope, evidence thresholds, consent/guardian process, student/tutor willingness to pay, content-review cost and pilot recruitment.

## Release gate
Teacher-reviewed content; validated diagnostic behavior; usable student/tutor journeys; privacy/security/consent review; functional/accessibility testing; pilot metrics and support process defined.
